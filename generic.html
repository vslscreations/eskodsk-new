Frequently Asked Questions

What is EskoDSK AI?

EskoDSK AI provides businesses with AI employees that can answer questions, capture leads, generate quotes, schedule services, and assist customers.

Is Avery just a chatbot?

No. Avery is designed to act like a virtual employee by understanding your business, guiding customers, and completing useful business tasks.

Can Avery be customized for my business?

Yes. Avery can be trained with your company's information, services, processes, and frequently asked questions.

Does this replace my employees?

No. EskoDSK AI helps your team by handling repetitive tasks and allowing employees to focus on higher-value work.
