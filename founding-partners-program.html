EskoDSK Launches Founding Partners Program

EskoDSK is introducing the Founding Partners Program, an opportunity for motivated developers, designers, and innovators to help build technology solutions that empower small businesses.

The program is designed for individuals who want hands-on experience creating real products while participating in the growth of an early-stage technology company.

Founding Partners will contribute their skills and expertise in areas such as software development, artificial intelligence, design, marketing, and business growth. This is an unpaid opportunity for time contributed; however, participants will be eligible for revenue participation based on the value they help create.

Partners who contribute to products, customer acquisition, or business growth will receive a percentage of revenue generated from their contributions. Long-term contributors who demonstrate exceptional commitment and impact may also be considered for future equity opportunities in EskoDSK.

This program is built for people who want more than experience. They want the opportunity to help shape a company from the beginning and share in its success.

EskoDSK is looking for ambitious individuals who are passionate about technology, entrepreneurship, and creating solutions that make small businesses more efficient and competitive.

Interested individuals are invited to e-mail us at Hello@eskodesk.com.
