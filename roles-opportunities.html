Contributor Roles & Development Opportunities

Overview

The EskoDSK Founding Partners Program is designed for motivated individuals who want to contribute to the development and growth of an early-stage technology company focused on empowering small businesses.

At the current stage of development, EskoDSK is seeking developers and technology-focused partners who can help build, improve, and scale the foundation of the platform.

The initial focus is software development, artificial intelligence integration, and building the systems required to create customizable digital solutions for small businesses.

As EskoDSK grows, the Founding Partners Program will expand to include contributors with expertise in additional areas such as user experience design, marketing, business development, operations, and other fields needed to build a successful technology company.

Founding Partners will not simply be assigned tasks. They will have the opportunity to contribute ideas, influence product direction, and help shape the future of EskoDSK.

Current Priority: Development & AI Partners

The immediate goal of EskoDSK is to build a strong technology foundation.

Founding Developers and AI Partners will help develop the systems behind EskoDSK products, including customizable client portals, business dashboards, workflow automation, and future virtual employee solutions.

Ideal candidates should have experience or interest in areas including:

  • Web application development
  • Artificial intelligence technologies
  • AI integrations and automation
  • API development
  • User experience development
  • Scalable software solutions

Future Areas of Growth

While developers and AI-focused contributors are the current priority, EskoDSK recognizes that building a successful technology company requires expertise across multiple disciplines.

Future Founding Partners will include:

Artificial Intelligence Specialists

Helping design and improve virtual employee technology, automation systems, and intelligent business solutions.

UI/UX Designers

Creating professional and intuitive experiences for business owners and their customers.

Marketing & Brand Specialists

Helping communicate the EskoDSK mission and connect with businesses that need better technology solutions.

Business Development Partners

Helping identify opportunities, build relationships, and expand into new industries.

Operations & Strategy Contributors

Helping create systems that allow EskoDSK to scale effectively.

Compensation & Partnership Model

EskoDSK is currently an early-stage company and does not operate through traditional employment structures.

Founding Partners contribute their expertise in exchange for participation in the success they help create.

The Founding Partners compensation model includes:

  • Revenue participation connected to products and solutions they directly help develop
  • Revenue participation connected to customers or partnerships they directly contribute to generating
  • Additional performance-based incentives tied to measurable impact
  • Long-term equity opportunities for Founding Partners who demonstrate continued commitment, leadership, and significant contribution to the growth of EskoDSK

Compensation structures will be clearly defined based on each contributor's role, responsibilities, and impact.

Who We Are Looking For

EskoDSK is looking for individuals who are:

  • Passionate about technology and innovation
  • Interested in artificial intelligence and the future of business automation
  • Excited by entrepreneurship and startup environments
  • Comfortable solving problems and creating new solutions
  • Interested in building technology that creates a real impact for small businesses

The ideal Founding Partner understands that early-stage companies require creativity, ownership, and collaboration.

Building the Future of Small Business Technology

EskoDSK is creating a platform designed to give small businesses access to the same type of technology advantages available to larger companies.

Founding Partners will have the opportunity to contribute to real products, solve meaningful problems, and help build a company from the earliest stages.

Together, we are building the future of accessible technology for small businesses.
